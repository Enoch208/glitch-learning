# Disclosures

## AI in the product

No model runs in GLITCH today. Diagnosis, rule execution, counterexamples, explanation checking, transfer and victory are all deterministic code. When a model is connected, it will be limited to proposing candidate rules in the closed rule language, judging free-text explanations and writing boss dialogue. It will not decide what is correct or whether the learner has won.

## AI-assisted development

The code was written with an AI coding assistant working under the entrant's direction. Every change went through the type check, linter, unit tests and production build, and user-facing changes were checked in a browser at phone width.

## Generated assets

The clay illustrations in `src/assets/clay` (the boss, the rule pieces, the learner avatar, the star badge, the backdrop and the background shapes) were generated by the entrant with an AI image generation tool. In this repository they were cropped, cleaned of background artefacts, and resized and compressed to WebP.

## Data

No real learner data was used to build or evaluate GLITCH. The evaluation uses seeded synthetic learners. The app stores nothing off the device.
